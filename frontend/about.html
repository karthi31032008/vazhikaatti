About VazhiKaatti

A community-focused digital platform designed to connect citizens with local services and information.

Who We Are

VazhiKaatti is a citizen-focused web platform that aims to make local information and community support easier to access.

It brings together local service information, community updates and public issue reporting in one convenient place.

Our Vision & Mission

🌍

Our Vision

To encourage informed, connected and active communities through digital technology.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible local information and make community participation simpler.

💡

Our Approach

Use simple technology to bring useful services and information together.
